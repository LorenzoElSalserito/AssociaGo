# 3. Activity Management

The **Activities & Coupons** page has two tabs: **Activities** (courses, workshops, seminars) and **Coupons** (see chapter 9).

## 3.1 Activity list

For each activity the table shows:
- name and category;
- period (start and end date);
- maximum number of participants, or "Unlimited" if there is no limit;
- status.

**Search activities...** filters by name. The actions on each row are **View details**, **Edit** and **Delete**.

## 3.2 Creating an activity

Press **Add Activity**.

### Main details
- **Activity Name** and **Category** are required. The categories are Course, Workshop, Seminar and Other.
- **Description** and **Location**.
- **Start Date** (required), **End Date**, **Start Time** and **End Time**.
- **Schedule Details**: free note, for example "Mon/Wed 6-8 pm".

### Registration and costs
- **Cost** and **Subscription Type**: One Time, Monthly or Annual.
- **Max Participants**: leave it empty if there is no limit.
- **Require Registration** and **Generate Invoice**.
- **Document Path/URL** and **Image Path/URL**, to attach material.

Press **Save Activity** to confirm.

## 3.3 Detail page

At the top, four indicators: **Participants**, **Revenue**, **Costs** and **Net Profit**. Below are six tabs.

### Participants
**Add Participant** opens the member list (**Select Member**) and an optional **Coupon code** field (see 9.3). The fee due is the activity cost minus any discount. The table shows each participant's status and payment (Paid, Pending) with the amount due and, if one was used, the coupon with its discount; it also lets you remove the participant.

In **Revenue**, fees not yet paid count for the amount due, already discounted.

### Schedule
**Add Schedule** records recurring lessons: day of the week, start and end time, location (optional).

### Instructors
**Add Instructor** records:
- first and last name (required), email, phone;
- **Specialization** and **Certifications** (e.g. CONI, FIGC);
- **Compensation Type** (Hourly, Fixed Rate or Percentage) and amount.

### Costs
**Add Cost** records the activity's expenses:
- description, amount and date (required);
- category: Materials, Equipment, Staff, Rent / Venue, Marketing, Other;
- supplier, **Recurring Cost** and notes.

Costs count towards Net Profit.

### Certificates
Choose a template and press **Issue Batch** to issue a certificate to every active participant enrolled as a member. Templates are created in the Certificates section (chapter 11).

### CSV Import
Import participants from a CSV file:
1. download the sample format;
2. press **Generate Preview**, which shows valid rows, skipped rows and errors;
3. press **Confirm Import**.

## 3.4 Report

The **Report** button downloads a summary PDF of the activity: main details and number of participants.
