# 8. Volunteer Management

Open **Volunteers** from the **People** group of the sidebar. Here you assign members the volunteer role, record shifts and manage expense reimbursements.

## 8.1 Volunteer list

For each volunteer the table shows:
- the member's name and email;
- skills;
- hours;
- status.

**Search volunteers...** filters by name. The actions on each row are **View details**, **Edit** and **Delete**.

The **Hours** column adds up the **Hours Worked** of all the volunteer's shifts; **Status** shows Active or Inactive.

## 8.2 Adding a volunteer

Press **Add Volunteer** and fill in:
- **Member** (required): choose the member from **Select a member...**;
- **Skills**: free text, e.g. First aid, Logistics, Communication;
- **Availability**: free text, e.g. Weekends, Evenings;
- **Status**: Active or Inactive;
- **Notes**.

Press **Create** (or **Update** when editing).

> When editing, the linked member does not change: to link a different member, create a new volunteer.

## 8.3 Detail page

The header shows **Volunteer** with its number and the **Member ID**, plus the **Edit** button. Three boxes show **Status**, **Skills** and **Availability**. Below are two tabs.

### Shifts
Press **Add Shift** and fill in:
- **Start Time** and **End Time** (date and time, required);
- **Role**;
- **Event (Optional)** or **Activity (Optional)**, to link the shift;
- **Hours Worked** and **Hourly Value (€)**;
- **Status**: Scheduled, Completed or Missed;
- **Description / Notes**.

Press **Save Shift**. The table shows start, end, role, hours and status; the edit icon reopens the shift.

> Hours worked are not calculated from the times: enter them yourself. The hourly value is saved but not used in any summary. When editing, only times, role, hours and status are updated; event, activity, hourly value and notes keep their original values. Shifts cannot be deleted.

### Expenses (expense reimbursements)
Press **Add Expense** and enter **Date**, **Description** and **Amount (€)**, all required, then **Save Expense**. The expense starts out pending approval.

The actions depend on the expense status:
- pending: **Approve** or **Reject**;
- approved: **Mark Paid**.

When you mark an expense as paid, AssociaGo automatically records an expense in Finance, with today's date and the Reimbursement category, under the association you are logged in with.

> Expenses cannot be edited or deleted, and receipts cannot be attached.
